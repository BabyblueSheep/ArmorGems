--==[[ OVERVIEW ]]==--
[Calamity] Armor Gems is a mod that lets you harness the power of armor within powerful "Armor Gem" accessories! After defeating the vile wizard Tim, a new face can appear to assist you in the creation of these powerful jewels. Discover over two dozen accessories, all with their own effect!

--==[[ COMPATABILITY ]]==--
No mod has been left unturned! [Calamity] Armor Gems features support for nearly every mod on the browser, thanks to how the mod was constructed. No need for addons in order to get a gem for your favorite mod! As long as your gems feature different prefixes, you can use as many as you wish.
